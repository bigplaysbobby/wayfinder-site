Wayfinder privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Wayfinder is a personal app used only by its owner on their own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it reads

When the owner connects their Google account, Wayfinder asks for read-only access to:

  • Google Calendar events, to show upcoming events.
  • Google Health data the owner chooses to share, such as activity, sleep, body measurements, nutrition and similar records.

Wayfinder never writes to, changes or deletes anything in the Google account.

Where the data goes

Data read from Google is stored only on the owner's computer, in an encrypted local database. It is not sent to any server run by Wayfinder, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising. Wayfinder's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google in Wayfinder's settings, or remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored Google sign-in token.
